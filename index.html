Projects

Numerical Modeling of a Vibration Isolation System (Parts 1–3)

Fitting & Differentiation: Fitted force-displacement data to both a third-order polynomial and a physics-guided nonlinear model. The physics model (SSE = 1687.9) fit better than the polynomial (SSE = 1716.6). Computed numerical derivatives using second-order central differences; verified O(h²) convergence with L2 error slope ≈ 2. Also integrated strain energy via trapezoidal (order ~2) and Simpson's rules (order ~4, converging to 1.288 N·m).

Numerical Modeling of a Vibration Isolation System (Parts 4–5)

Solver & Frequency Analysis: Modeled forced vibration using MATLAB's adaptive ODE45 solver (min dt = 4.15e-3 s, mean = 1.03e-2 s). Confirmed tolerance independence (max diff = 2.4e-5 m) and prepared uniform time grid for FFT at fs ≈ 500 Hz. Frequency response showed a dominant peak at 2.5 Hz (magnitude 3.04e-2) and secondary at 7 Hz (1.41e-3), demonstrating the system acts as a low-pass vibration filter.
