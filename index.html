🧮 Physics Foundations

Moment of Inertia (Eq. 1) I = Σ(mᵢ × rᵢ²)

Higher MOI reduces rotational penalty on off-center hits

Dynamic Loft (Eq. 2) θ_dynamic = θ_static + Δθ_deflection

Minimizing dynamic loft variation reduces three-putt frequency

Gravitational Torque (Eq. 3) τ_gravity = m × g × r_CG × sin(φ)

Optimizing initial backspin shortens skid-roll transition

🔬 Materials & Manufacturing

Club Head

Material: 6061 Aluminum (Industry Standard)

Rationale: Balance of cost efficiency and machinability. Provides exceptional feel while striking the ball.

Tolerance: ±0.005 in precision machining

Weighting System

Material: 304 Stainless Steel

Rationale: Corrosion resistance for outdoor exposure. Originally 303 considered, but 304 provides better weather protection.

Implementation: Distributed to club perimeter to maximize MOI

Surface Finish

Roughness: 125 µin (2.3 µm) Ra minimum

Treatment: Black Anodize coating

Edge Treatment: All sharp edges broken, burrs removed

⚡ What is Zero Torque Design?

The Zero Torque design is balanced in all directions around the shaft. When the putter is balanced on the shaft regardless of orientation, it will not self-correct because one side of the club always has the same weight as the opposite side. This design ensures that:

  • The shaft intersects with the center of gravity — critical for eliminating unwanted rotation
  • Center-shafted placement provides maximum comfort and accuracy (validated at Golf Galaxy)
  • Symmetrical weight distribution eliminates torque-induced face rotation during stroke
