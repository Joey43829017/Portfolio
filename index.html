About

Biography

Joseph Kozlowski is a dedicated Mechanical Engineering student at The Ohio State University with a passion for quality and testing. His academic journey has been marked by hands-on experience in thermodynamics, system dynamics, kinematics, and machine design. As an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, he actively supports fellow students while deepening his understanding of core engineering principles.

Beyond the classroom, Joseph brings curiosity and creativity to every project—from designing custom golf putters with precision physics-based engineering to building Arduino prototypes that demonstrate practical control systems applications. His work reflects a commitment to excellence in both analytical thinking and hands-on implementation.

Career Interests

Engineering Specialties: Thermodynamics, System Dynamics, Kinematics and Mechanism Design, Machine Design, Machining, Microcontrollers (Arduino IDE)

Professional Mission: To develop innovative mechanical systems that solve real-world problems while maintaining the highest standards of engineering quality and technical excellence.
